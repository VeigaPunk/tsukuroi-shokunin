## tool-environment caveat (NOT host evidence)
This seat's bash wrapper claims 'mktemp is a shell builtin' at top level and answers --version with 'mktemp 0.8.0'.
Under sh -c / bash -c / bash --noprofile --norc the same token resolves to /usr/bin/mktemp (GNU coreutils 9.12).
All mktemp behaviour above was therefore re-run through 'env -i PATH=/usr/bin:/bin sh -c ...'.
