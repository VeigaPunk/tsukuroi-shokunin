## Proposed criteria

Quality 93 does not require a results-file membership check when the verdict comes from the seat's own file. Quality 91 says "a counted seat", not "a seat other than the one that demanded the correction". Quality 32 binds the ten outbound pointer rows; at this revision its cited `:46-52` are the handoff cells, which until this commit held bare ranges.

94. **A report-bound verdict still requires the orchestrator's completed results row (R49-RESULTS-MEMBER):** a counter that takes a counted verdict from a file the seat writes must also require exactly one results entry for that seat in the declared wave's orchestrator results file, with the declared agent role and a non-empty output. A report with no such entry, or an entry whose output is empty, credits nothing. The pre-data controls include a cohort whose report files exist and whose results entries do not, and that cohort must fail. A name-and-role count that does not read output is not this check. Failing example: `tools/check-scrub-admission-r48.py:189-191` counts a results row and never reads `output`; `tools/check-scrub-admission-r49.py:275-281` refuses an empty output, and the freeze record's C1 K6 is reports present with results entries absent. Lesson: round 49, `LOG.md:1706-1712`.

95. **A correction a counted seat demanded is re-read by a new cohort (R49-NEW-COHORT):** when a counted verdict names a defect in a row, the corrected bytes are re-read only by seats that did not demand that correction. The counter's universe is that new cohort; a report from a demanding seat credits nothing toward the corrected row. Quality 91's "a counted seat" does not say this.
