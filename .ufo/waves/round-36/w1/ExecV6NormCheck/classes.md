# Round-35 v6 normalization debt classes

## What the retained evidence establishes

- `byte-invariance.txt:3-18` records 16 unchanged tool files between the compared revisions, including every target in both result sets. The v6 positives therefore came from probe/fixture behavior, not target-byte changes.
- `r33claim-v6.jsonl:17` records 5 targets, 3 channels, 15 pairs, and 8 leaking pairs. Its per-pair rows (`:2-16`) attribute these positives to `base-parity` only: eight names/quiet pairs across four targets. Their evidence is a different `git_argv_sha256`; the record does not retain the argv strings that formed that digest.
- `regression-v6.jsonl:35` records 11 targets, 3 channels, 33 pairs, and 22 leaking pairs. `regression-clause-summary.txt:1-33` shows `any-name-value` on names/quiet for nine targets (18 pairs), and `base-parity` on freshness plus the two owed-statements mutants (6 pairs, overlapping freshness's two any-name-value pairs). Thus 22 distinct pairs leaked.
- In the regression rows, the explicit any-name-value key is `GIT_CEILING_DIRECTORIES`; there are no retained `values_reached` or injected-name hits on these byte-identical controls. For example, `regression-v6.jsonl` rows 2-3 record the key for `check-adjacency-overlap.py`; rows 5-6 record it alongside base-parity for `check-freshness-r17.py`.
- The old probe's `anyname_carriers` checks whether a child value contains run-private HOME/XDG/injection tokens or equals TMPDIR (`probe-child-env-allowlist-r34.py:526-538`). Its argv normalizer only replaced the full run paths, exact 40/64-hex tokens, and the first component below TMPDIR (`:379-385`; regexes at `:192-194`).
- The round-36 declaration explicitly calls for normalizing “short hashes, fixture paths and GIT_CEILING_DIRECTORIES/$HOME-derived values” (SCORECARD.md, “Round 36 — declaration”, “Counter and freeze (C1)”). These are the value-shape classes represented below.

In particular, the v6 summaries do not show a value-channel `NAME=VALUE` match, an injected-name match, an execveat hit, or an unparseable exec record on the byte-identical false positives. The three base-parity shape attributions above are candidate classes guided by the frozen round-36 debt statement, not recoverable token-by-token facts; the digest-only evidence is a limitation, not proof that each one occurred in each affected pair.
