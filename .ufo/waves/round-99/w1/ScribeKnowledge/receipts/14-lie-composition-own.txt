The wrapper-side failures are mechanical: the wrapper does out = json.loads(p.stdout)
before it can falsify a field, and a refusal answers rc 64 with an EMPTY stdout, so json.loads("")
raises and the wrapper exits 1; the refusal family then sees rc 1 where it expects rc 64.
