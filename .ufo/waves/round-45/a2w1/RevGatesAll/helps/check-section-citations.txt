usage: check-section-citations.py [-h] (--revision REV | --selftest)
                                  [--branch BRANCH]

Narrow read-only round-16 section-citation errata gate.

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --revision REV   required: a 7-64 hex commit; reads immutable objects only
                   (HEAD/@/@-/ref names rejected: under jj HEAD is @-)
  --selftest       in-memory fixture suite plus a mktemp git binding sandbox
  --branch BRANCH  the revision and baseline must be ancestors of
                   refs/heads/<BRANCH> and of refs/remotes/origin/<BRANCH>
                   when it exists; default master
