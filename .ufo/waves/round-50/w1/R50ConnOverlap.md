Pin quote intent overlaps anchors.py's snapshot quote check, but the byte authority differs: checker validates the lane snapshot/line and counts the sibling pinned-blob hash; C3 re-derives content from the sibling repository and fails on a digest mismatch. **Keep both**: C3 closes the self-consistent snapshot+registry rewrite gap and supplies a real HEAD quote check plus stub sensitivity.

No whole check is redundant. One clear common substrate is `PROVINCE.md`: the three tools validate different sections/registers there, so edits need section-aware regression checks.
