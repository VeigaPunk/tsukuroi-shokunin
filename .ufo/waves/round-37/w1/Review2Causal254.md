## Findings

- **C1 — operability: axis fit is not demonstrated by the declared outcome.** The target is described as a static-analysis probe/battery (`SCORECARD.md:1867-1904`), and its after predicate is `leaking == 0`, empty errors and exit 0 on five tools (`:1884-1902`). The cited behavior demonstrates a source-analysis battery result, not that the checklist/probe is runnable against an operational host surface or that it produces the stated operability outcome beyond this one fixed set. `AXES.md:13` defines operability as runnable artifacts where a checklist item can be run and produces its stated outcome; `AXES.md:27-35` requires a verifiable axis improvement. To support the declared operability claim, bind the counter to observable execution of the required livefive invocation and its result for each target, rather than treating the analysis predicate alone as proof of operability. This is a concrete evidence/axis-fit gap, not a claim that the probe is defective.

- **C2 — depth: “correction-complete” is bounded to three selected defects, not completeness of the routed list.** The declaration calls the note “correction-complete per the closure's routed list” but then defines its counter as only three assertions: 15 terms, the `-n` distinction, and action-override classification (`SCORECARD.md:1906-1919`). No predicate checks that the entire routed list is represented or that the “rejection line stays verbatim” (`:1910-1914`). Thus an omitted or altered rejection line can still produce exit 0, although the declaration explicitly includes its preservation in the before/after contract. `AXES.md:11` ties depth to verifiable technical detail and cited sources; `CORPUS.md:26` requires falsifiable knowledge entries. Add a rejection-preservation assertion (and make the routed-list scope explicit) or do not describe the result as correction-complete.

- **C3 — calibration: the pre-freeze correction is not causally separable from C2.** The checker population includes K07–K11 stale-count checks per the declaration (`SCORECARD.md:1958-1968` identifies K07–K11 as the five mismatches), while C2 changes the journalctl knowledge note and adds a checker, and the declared change sequence also adds a lesson and other corpus/SKILL artifacts (`:1943-1948`). Those changes can alter the very counts C3 repairs; therefore the C3 values cannot be frozen independently of C2's edits, contrary to the declaration's claim that “only the self-assessment values may change” (`:1965-1968`) and the separability requirement in `AXES.md:30-32`. The subsequent C3 correction fixes the ledger proposal but does not resolve this dependency. Pin C3 measurement after the relevant C1/C2 content is fixed, or adjust its scope/claim-separability statement.
