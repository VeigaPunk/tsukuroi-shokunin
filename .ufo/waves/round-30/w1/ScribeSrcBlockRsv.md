8. [inf] Size formula in plain words, from those lines: the reserve size is the smaller of 512 MiB and the larger of (a) the summed used bytes of the root tree, extent tree, checksum tree and free-space tree, plus the block-group tree when that feature is on and the raid-stripe tree when that feature is on, and (b) the metadata-insert cost of one item per counted tree plus six unlink units, plus the delayed-reference cost of those same six unlink units. The reserve's `reserved` counter is then set to that size and the difference is accounted in `bytes_may_use`, which is why the figure is held space rather than free space. Context beyond the function: lines 60-67 call `BLOCK_RSV_GLOBAL` an overflow buffer for extent-tree updates that can also be stolen from on evict/truncate to recover from ENOSPC, and lines 564-571 call it "precious space" kept for updates the transaction did not reserve, with lines 586-593 falling back to `global_rsv` only when normal reservation fails.
