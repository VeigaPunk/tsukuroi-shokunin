83. **A post-wave verdict change is grounded per declared conjunct, and a disagreeing closure re-measures the conjuncts (R38-VERDICT-CONJUNCT):** when a round files a declared claim `not verified` (or otherwise against its declared after-state), or a non-author closure changes that verdict, the verdict line names, for every declared conjunct of that claim, exactly one ground class — a counter receipt at the declared revision reading FAIL (downgrade) or PASS (upgrade), an explicit alternative measurement of that conjunct at the declared revision (command and observed output) where no counter asserts it, or the fired kill rule with its receipt; findings outside the declared conjuncts — extensions beyond them, cross-claim coupling, a design defect — are routed as next-round debt and never move the verdict by themselves, a closure upgrade states per conjunct the command run and the observed output, and the author's filed verdict text is preserved in place. The disagreement is recorded in the round's lesson with a Mistakes row, and that lesson grounds this criterion.
