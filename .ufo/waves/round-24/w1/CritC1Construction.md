9. [inf] "Repair of the routed defects" is not an honest label. Lines 52 and 64 of the spot-check are constraints and routing for the next round, not defects in round-23 bytes. But the before-to-after framing in finding 1 cannot be fixed with code: the canonical judge SHOULD reject a calibration delta of 0 to 268 whose counting command returns 268 at the before revision, unless the claim is restated as a one-time content claim about a frozen cohort.

## Smallest safe correction

- Pin the frozen cohort. Require the four input sha256 values to equal those of the `f169b3ba` blobs, and require `total_findings` 268 with per-id counts 266, 1 and 1, before `preserved` can be true. This alone kills M2 through M6, because each changes checker or events bytes.
- For each event-level finding, re-classify the joined event with that seat's binding and require the finding id in the result. For each seat-level finding, require the site to be a bound seat whose trace is incomplete or missing, taking incompleteness from the checker result. Add M2 through M6, and the `request_line` join, as preregistered sandbox controls that MUST flip `preserved` to false.
- Emit per-finding rows: site, `request_line`, `call_id`, tool, finding id, and an observational sub-cause. The sub-causes are query suffix on a declared file, query suffix on an undeclared file, glob pattern, other fleet path, relative path, and lane-local undeclared. Every finding id stays unchanged and red. Mark ConnFxPlanner rows as coming from an incomplete trace.
- Restate the target as "267 event-level findings joined to a recorded request plus 1 seat-level finding, over pinned frozen inputs". Drop the measured 0-to-268 delta and the word "repair", or rename the claim to "per-cause report honouring routed constraints".
