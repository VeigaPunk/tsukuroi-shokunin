## Attack
Re-open the R17 `/new_root` symlink/path-resolution evidence. It is falsifiable if the pinned captures do not show all four 42.2 images linking `new_root -> sysroot`, if the 41.1 image is not a real directory, or if the recorded `findmnt`/`mount --move`/OverlayFS sequence fails to resolve and land on `/sysroot`. Also cross-check the relevant citations and scope limits.

## Method
- Read the target-revision copies of `.ufo/waves/round-17/l1-capture/overlay-emulation.txt`, `boot-chain-capture.txt`, `corpus/knowledge/boot-chain-r17.md`, and `corpus/knowledge/initramfs-v42-sysroot-recovery.md`. Compared their working-file SHA-256 values with `git show 8e5916360f2dfe7bc4eb4ba144081af555900116:<path> | sha256sum`; all four matched.
- Inspected the retained extracted trees under `/tmp/tsukuroi-r17-boot`, listed the initrds with `lsinitcpio -v`, compared paired initrd hashes, and read the installed `functions`, `init`, and `btrfs-overlayfs` code and the mkinitcpio mtree.
- Re-ran the capture block (overlay-emulation lines 23–39) in a private `unshare -rm --propagation private` user+mount namespace, from `/tmp`, after removing only its transcript `$ ` prefix. Also ran a control with `ln -s sysroot new_root` replaced by `mkdir new_root`. The first attempt before stripping the prompt failed with `command not found: $` (rc 127); it is not treated as a test result.
- No host boot, `/boot` access, package action, service action, or installation was performed.

## Verdict
**CONFIRM — R17-SYMLINK path-resolution evidence.** The four 42.2 image types and the 41.1 directory match the captures; the successful namespace reproduction matches the recorded path sequence and its limits. One earlier citation-scope mismatch in the recovery notice is explicitly identified and corrected by its appended erratum; it remains visible in the original notice text.
