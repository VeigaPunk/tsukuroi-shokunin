=== AUDIT OF QUALITY 80-93 AGAINST CONTENT DIGEST BINDING ===

QUALITY 80 (R31-DEPTH-UNFALSIFIABLE):
  Requires do-nothing controls and novelty sweeps as facts.
  Scope: Target falsifiability before data.
  Gap: Does not bind reviewer verdicts to content digests.

QUALITY 81 (R31-SCRIPTED-NONREADER):
  Requires non-author reviewer verdict file and scripted writer control.
  Scope: Independent reviewer participation.
  Gap: Requires format (holds, partly, fails, source lines checked), but does not
  require cryptographic binding to the section bytes read.

QUALITY 82 (R32-FIXTURE-GATEABLE):
  Requires behavioural counters to score code outside fixtures (live host).
  Scope: Fixture evasion defense.
  Gap: Unrelated to verdict/identity content binding.

QUALITY 83 (R38-VERDICT-CONJUNCT):
  Requires post-wave verdict change to be grounded per declared conjunct.
  Scope: Verdict revision discipline.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 84 (R38-ECHO-UNVALIDATED):
  One-line machine grammar echoes only validated tokens.
  Scope: Serialization safety.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 85 (R38-Q82-LEAK):
  Production-path view built inside child mount namespace.
  Scope: Namespace isolation.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 86 (round-38 C2 counter gaps):
  Rule row verifies on upstream source row, never own-work capture alone.
  Scope: Evidence provenance.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 87 (R39-CONTENT-UNREAD):
  Content counter reads content it credits (rules/propositions), with content-free control.
  Scope: Counter inspection of text cells.
  Gap: Governs the counter's parser, not the reviewer's verdict or script's identity attestation.

QUALITY 88 (R40-CLAIM-PARAMETERS):
  Counter acceptance implies declaration parameters; single-parameter violation control.
  Scope: Parameter completeness.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 89 (R41-GUARD-BYPASS):
  Adversarial guard scored against evasion defeating guard mechanism.
  Scope: Adversarial testing of guard mechanisms.
  Gap: Broad principle; does not mandate content digest binding for verdicts.

QUALITY 90 (R42-OBSERVED-CHANNEL):
  Counter scored against observation channels; verdict binding matches asserted line, not mention.
  Scope: Channel covertness and regex anchoring.
  Gap: Matches line syntax ("asserted line"), not content hash.

QUALITY 91 (R42-ADOPTED-UNREAD):
  Integrator checks source lines; counted read follows every adopted change.
  Scope: Integrator discipline when adopting reviewer notes.
  Gap: Prescribes manual operational re-reading, but provides NO mechanical enforcement,
  no digest declaration in seat reports, and no counter verification of digests.

QUALITY 92 (R43-CAP-HEADER-DUP):
  Rendered table carries exactly one header row.
  Scope: Markdown table formatting.
  Gap: Unrelated to content digest binding.

QUALITY 93 (R45-C2-BINDING-CHANNEL):
  Counted verdict binds through channel counted seat writes verbatim; controls score actual yield shapes.
  Scope: Transmission channel selection (seat report vs orchestrator results) and yield parsing.
  Gap: Binds the carrier/channel, NOT the semantic content. Allows a verdict on row GR2
  to bind to row GR2 even if GR2's content was rewritten or replaced after review.

CONCLUSION:
  A clear architectural gap exists: while QUALITY 91 notes that adopted changes must be re-read
  and QUALITY 93 governs the verbatim reporting channel, neither requires cryptographic content
  binding between the verdict/identity and the exact evaluated bytes. QUALITY 94 is warranted.
