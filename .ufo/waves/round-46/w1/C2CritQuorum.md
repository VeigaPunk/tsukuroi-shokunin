## Smallest safe next-round rule to freeze

For each row, freeze one candidate-section SHA-256 before dispatch. Admit only when at least two distinct counted seats bind `holds` to that exact hash, including at least one reviewer and one critic; any bound `fails` on that hash blocks admission. Preserve and report all earlier-hash verdicts as history, but do not treat them as verdicts on the new bytes. If the candidate changes after any counted verdict is observed, label it a new candidate revision, freeze its hash before further review, and require the full cross-role quorum afresh; retain prior hashes/verdicts and record the change rationale. Pre-data controls must demonstrate same-role-only holds fail, cross-role holds pass, a current-hash fail blocks, stale-hash `partly` neither vetoes nor counts, and post-verdict revision cannot reuse old quorum. This adds role diversity and prevents silent quorum shopping while respecting the bytes each seat actually reviewed.
