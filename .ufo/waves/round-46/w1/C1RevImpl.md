## Finding 1 — identity accepts `--help` alongside the exclusive flag

`tools/tsukuroi-census.sh:3495-3518` records `nargs` before parsing but handles `--help` by calling `usage; exit 0` inside the parse loop (`:3508`), before the exclusive `nargs == 1` check. Observed scratch-copy command: `bash census.sh --identity --help` exits 0 and prints usage, contrary to the stated contract that `--identity` takes no other argument and should exit 2; `--identity --identity` and `--identity --sample 1` reach the check and exit 2. Exact fix: in the `--help | -h)` arm, if `identity` is 1, print the `--identity takes no other argument` error and usage to stderr, then exit 2; alternatively defer help handling until after validating that identity is the sole original argument. Keep standalone `--help` behavior unchanged.

## Finding 2 — identity selftest expected-plane extraction is brittle under `pipefail`

At `tools/tsukuroi-census.sh:3394`, `id_check` uses `grep | head -n1 | grep | sed | paste`. `run_selftest` operates under `set -euo pipefail` (`:2046-2048`), and the command substitution is unguarded. If the match line is absent/changed, grep failure aborts the entire selftest rather than recording an assertion failure; if the first grep emits multiple lines, `head` may close the pipe and cause upstream SIGPIPE (141), also aborting under `pipefail`. Exact fix: derive the expected plane field from a fixed independent literal matching `CENSUS_PLANES`, or make extraction guarded and SIGPIPE-free and report a normal failed assertion when extraction is empty. The implementation currently emits six PLANE records and rolls up exactly those six at `:3543-3548`, `:3580-3589`; its `CENSUS_PLANES` literal at `:3465` currently matches the final rollup fields.
