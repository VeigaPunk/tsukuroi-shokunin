--- 7. WHAT THIS SOURCE IS AND IS NOT FOR THE LANE -------------------------
It is the upstream statement of the CPython int<->str decimal-conversion digit limit (4300 by
default, 640 floor) and of the ValueError raised past it. It is the documented reason the gate
refuses a version component before handing it to int(): `tools/check-limine-pins.py`
`parse_limine_version()` (line 92-101) caps each component at 9 digits and its docstring says
"A component longer than 9 digits is refused rather than handed to int(), which raises above 4300
digits (round-92 ReviewGate F1)".
It is NOT a Limine source and NOT a statement about Limine's own version format; Limine sources
are separate rows (S386/S387/S388, re-opened in receipt-02).
