## 5. Flags carried to the report

5.1 Locator drift (kotoba → tsukuroi). kotoba/PROVINCE.md:78 quotes a tsukuroi proposition and pins
    "PROVINCE.md:22". At 1fac4195, tsukuroi PROVINCE.md:22 is the "Initramfs v42 and boot recovery" row;
    the phrase "all read-only probes" lives on PROVINCE.md:8, not :22. The proposition (tsukuroi owns host
    package + initramfs hooks, read-only) is true in substance; the citation mixes a bare line number with a
    paraphrase — the locator class tsukuroi's own round-49 note (R49) retired for its own table. Report to
    kotoba; do not edit.

5.2 Digest-domain boundary (chousa ↔ this round's surface). chousa r67 refuses a "blake2" digest as
    `NO_DIGESTS` in a receipt. The r88 surface reads a BLAKE2b-512 pin out of limine.conf and (C2) quotes it
    as host evidence. Neither text is wrong; a receiver that applies one rule to the other's artefact will
    read a correct pin as invalid, or accept an un-derivable receipt. This is the same confusion that produced
    the r87 erratum (ScoutHost labelled the BLAKE2b-512 column "sha256", routed defect 5). Named, not resolved
    here: the L1 decides whether the r88 entry needs one sentence fixing the digest domains.

5.3 Uncited sibling evidence. mado's pacman-log reading of the 2026-10-20 09:15 `-Syu` (mkinitcpio 41.1→42.2)
    and mori's closed-handoff status for the same boot-chain window are the only sibling rows stating the
    preconditions the r88 host state rests on; neither is cited by the r88 receipts I read. Adjacent, no
    conflict.
