config_get_entry returns a pointer to the '\n' that ends entry 0's line (config.c 704-720), so the
walk-back at 645-647 lands on entry 0's leading '/'. Line 649 then NULs the character before that
'/', i.e. the newline that ends the last global line. Every later config_get_value(NULL, ...) scan
(menu.c 1711 hash_mismatch_panic, menu.c 1720 measured_boot) therefore stops at that NUL and sees
the global section only — UNLESS entry 0 starts at offset 0, where the `c > config_addr` guard at
648 skips the write and the whole file (entry bodies included) remains visible to the scan.
