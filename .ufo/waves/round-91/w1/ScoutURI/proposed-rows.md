## Limits of this seat's contribution

- Nothing was booted and no host file was written. The host statements are read from the committed
  receipt `.ufo/waves/round-91/before/host/receipt.txt` only.
- The harness compiles `uri_resolve` **alone** (lines 32-107 of the pinned `uri.c`) with upstream's
  `URI_ERROR`/`panic` semantics re-implemented as a stub. It therefore exercises the resolver's own
  branching exactly, but it is not the whole bootloader: the `uri_open` dispatch (UR9-UR13) and the
  `chainload` wrapper (UR16-UR20) were read, not executed.
- `common/entry.s3.c` (UR23) and `common/lib/panic.s2.c` (UR24) were read from the tag clone but are
  **not** pinned in `.ufo/waves/round-91/after/src-c2/` this round; their sha256 is recorded in
  `receipt-uri-resolve.txt` §1 for a future pin.
- The host receipt's `path:` lines for the six efi entries are **truncated at 140 columns**
  (`receipt-gate-observations.txt` §6). Lines 30 and 38 show 93 and 89 hash characters where a real
  128-hex hash needs 128; lines 50/56/64 show no `#` only because the line is cut. **Only line 72
  (37 columns, no `#`) is complete.** So the receipt cannot by itself establish that lines 30/38/50/56/64
  carry a hash, and this seat does not assert that they do — the r90 receipt's `LP-MISMATCH line 30` /
  `LP-MISMATCH line 38` findings (which require a hash to compare against) are the stronger evidence,
  and the truncation is the reason the two disagree on their face.
- The "no `#`" case is the one C2 names; the frozen battery's U1 rule and the frozen corpus text both
  already treat it as an "unverified image line". This seat's contribution is the *mechanism* (UR5-UR13,
  UR25) and the consequence split (UR18-UR19, UR26-UR29), not a re-scoping of the rule set.
