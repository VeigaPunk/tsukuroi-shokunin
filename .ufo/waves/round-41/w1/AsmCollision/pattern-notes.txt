Pattern used (row-anchored, 0-3 leading spaces, opening and closing table pipe):
  P03='^ {0,3}\|[[:space:]]*(S[0-9]{3})[[:space:]]*\|'
Guard control (column zero only):
  P0='^\|[[:space:]]*(S[0-9]{3})[[:space:]]*\|'

Pattern equivalence on the real files (rows counted with grep -cE):
  SOURCES.md@base 0e5805a1  0to3=226  col0=226
  SOURCES.md@c2   9cc9aa77  0to3=236  col0=236
  SOURCES.append.md         0to3=147  col0=147
=> the 0-3-space allowance is a guard for indented rows, not a behaviour change on today's files.

Bound control: an indented row at 4 spaces is NOT captured by P03 (0 matches), so the allowance is bounded.
