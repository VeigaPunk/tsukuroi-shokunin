102. **A declaration freezes only receipt-bound premises, and qualitative conjuncts name their deciding measurement (R69-DECLARATION-HYGIENE):** before a declaration row is frozen, every location, count and digest it names carries, in the same round's committed `before/` receipts, the command and its observed output that produced it. A qualitative conjunct ("outside X", "the five", "unchanged") names the measurement that decides it and its bound; an unmeasured conjunct is not a frozen fact. A number that no receipt prints is replaced by the predicate it was meant to express or removed, never supplied from recollection. Negative examples, respectively: round 68's `oom_kill 1507 → 1533` mixes a description line with a label (the label is at `1506 → 1533`); "the changed hunks ... outside the memory-controller section" has no pre-freeze section-attribution measurement and is falsified by one Memory hunk among sixteen; "the five v7.2-pinned registry rows" has no enumeration producing five for that definite scope, while R1Reviewer enumerates eleven, so it must instead name the selected row ids or the intended scope predicate. `UNCHANGED` is decided by equality of the named pinned byte pair, not by an unchanged anchor alone. The retrospective checklist must retain both receipt-backed premises and failures; later review evidence diagnoses a missing pre-freeze receipt but cannot retroactively supply it. Frozen predicates are not amended after data.
