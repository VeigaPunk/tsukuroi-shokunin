# Admission bar (probation draft)

1. Every claim falsifiable or marked opinion.
2. Every claim carries a source: a measured anchor (path:line, command,
   evidence id) — never oral knowledge.
3. Observation, inference, and unknown stay separate.
4. Errata are append-only blocks.
5. A rejection is kept, not silently dropped.

6. Before advising any operator pacman action, cite ArchWiki System maintenance S010; bare `pacman -Sy` is never advised (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes D1/MC1).
7. External claims cite an exact `corpus/SOURCES.md` S### row, its tier and applicable section anchor; cite effective local configuration rather than an inactive/default file; tier C is never sole support, with tier definitions in SOURCES.md (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes D5).
8. Before retaining an [unknown], sweep the relevant local log/journal and readable upstream source/man page and record the sweep; every knowledge entry has `source:`, `verified-at:`, and `recheck:` with an ISO date or a cadence (`<N>d` or `on-release`) (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes D2/MC2, M13).
9. Every negative claim includes its bounded search/probe sweep command, scope and date (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes M3).
10. Before a correction commit, sweep every mention in README.md, SKILL.md and corpus/knowledge/ for superseded text, and verify changed Markdown tables retain their intended column count and delimiters (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes M8, M12).
11. The landing commit contains its matching LOG.md entry with motivating evidence (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes M11).
12. An absent tool does not make its mechanism unknown: read and cite its upstream source or man page before tagging [inference] (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes D4/MC4).
13. EACCES triage records target mode/owner/ACL and the unit's CapabilityBoundingSet/ProtectSystem; because -Qkk cannot see /, the census separately reports root-directory mode (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-1.md ## Mistakes D3/MC3, D6).

14. Before recording an admit for a new knowledge entry, require its explicit `## QUALITY check` to evaluate the applicable numbered criteria and resolve its source/evidence pointers; a promised check is not a performed check (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-12.md ## Mistakes, outside round-1 omission).
15. Root metadata findings report owner-write/search bits, not universal mode policy or process writability. Permission-denial explanations distinguish inode DAC/ACL, mount namespace and runtime credentials; ordinary `mkdir` controls never certify a historical Btrfs ioctl cause (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-12.md ## Mistakes, ordering and permission-proxy errors).
16. Before grading metadata as unhealthy, inspect the owning policy sources and overrides, including tmpfiles rules; an unowned inode is not a policy-free inode. Known configured mode differences are observations, not automatic drift findings (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-12.md ## Mistakes, package-baseline versus policy-baseline).

17. A unit-permission explanation that uses “only”, “not configured”, or an exhaustive mechanism list must scope that conclusion to the properties actually checked. Compare ProtectSystem values without excluding BindReadOnlyPaths, TemporaryFileSystem mount options, or an unobserved private path view; RootDirectory/RootImage are path-view changes, not inherently read-only. A structural counter cannot waive source-inconsistent prose. Evaluate the active superseding entry and retain the rejected historical record (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-13.md ## Mistakes, R13-DAC-EXCLUSIVITY; outside round-12 C2 rejection).
18. Label root-owned hypothetical scenarios as source-grounded inference when executed controls used caller-owned fixtures; record bot comments as automated community triage, never maintainer acceptance. Preserve immutable source fields and append superseding rows (lesson: corpus/lessons/round-13.md ## Mistakes, R13-FIXTURE and R13-BOT).

19. **Claim/source scope (R14-SOURCE-SCOPE):** for each admitted replacement sentence, name the source object, its located ≤25-word quote and the exact proposition it supports. An option's documented value domain is not the open set of mechanisms; account fields, a comment's execution assertion, a model second opinion and maintainer acceptance are different propositions. Never transfer a disclaimer across comments. The same bounded correction check must fail the preserved prior defects and restored-defect fixtures before this fix counts; a passing structural count does not establish source agreement. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-14.md` § Mistakes, R14-VALUE-DOMAIN and R14-COMMENT-ATTRIBUTION; outside finding `round-13-verifier-spotcheck.md:143-165`.
20. **Supersedes criterion 18's blanket bot attribution only:** identify the exact comment by ID; report verified API fields separately from what its body claims. Do not call #8047/comment6071740243 self-described model-generated automated triage: its body asserts VM execution and labels Codex Medium a second opinion. Treat that execution as unverified and retain the host-cause rejection; an account name is not proof of generation method or maintainer acceptance. Criterion 18's fixture-identity rule remains. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-14.md` § Mistakes, R14-COMMENT-ATTRIBUTION; superseding source row S045.

21. **Portable locators (R15-LOCATOR):** use a stable source heading/fragment, exact quote and observed revision or access date. Supplemental reader line numbers require the renderer and source snapshot; otherwise withdraw them as locators without withdrawing the true located proposition. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-15.md` § Mistakes; outside `round-14-verifier-spotcheck.md` § Adjudicated concerns item1.
22. **Positive definitions versus absence (R15-POSITIVE-DOMAIN):** a quoted option definition supports its stated argument forms. Do not append “documents no further values” or an exhaustive parser claim unless its own bounded command, scope and date are recorded; an unrelated comment-body sweep is insufficient. Prefer the supported positive proposition. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-15.md` § Mistakes; outside `round-14-verifier-spotcheck.md` § Adjudicated concerns item2.

23. **Section/table citation resolution (R16-SECTION):** before admitting a citation repair, resolve the exact heading and uniquely identified table row in the cited revision, then compare the quoted row text. A correct quote does not excuse a wrong section number. Preserve prior notices and append a named citation-only erratum; do not refresh historical host observations by repairing their locator. Require actual wrong-section red findings and independently fixed restored-defect cases. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-16.md` § Mistakes, R16-BOARD-SECTION and R16-RANK-SECTION; outside `round-15-verifier-spotcheck.md` § Routed defects items 1–2.
24. **Execution/evidence scope (R16-SEAT-SCOPE):** a read-only assignment is not permission for extra package/log/ownership probes, isolated matcher executions or syntax compilation. Wave seats obey the narrower explicit no-execution/no-child contract; only L1 performs authorized verification after integration. Record deviations as deviations, never as approved verification. The build/falsify requirement is demonstrated by dispatched registries and delivered attack reports, not a prospective lesson sentence. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-16.md` § Mistakes, R16-FALSIFY and R16-SEAT-SCOPE; outside `round-15-verifier-spotcheck.md` § Routed defects items 3–4.

25. **Next-boot verdicts from images, typed (R17-IMAGE-TYPED):** a next-boot verdict is derived from the extracted boot image (init root references, hook root paths, digests) bound to the entry and image that actually booted (firmware loader variables, image hash), never from `/etc` configuration or package versions alone. A path-existence fact inside an image records the entry type (`ls -l` / `lsinitcpio -v`), because a type-blind listing cannot tell a directory from a symlink. A predicted failure mode needs a mechanism run (emulation or boot) before it is filed as an operator decision. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-17.md` § Mistakes, R17-TYPE-BLIND.
26. **Execution scope binds the L1 too (R17-STEER-SCOPE):** the round's narrowest command scope (a steer's "never run pacman" beats a generic transaction pause) binds every executor, the L1 included. The L1 copies the forbidden binaries into its capture header and substitutes file reads for them (for example `/var/lib/pacman/local/<pkg>-<ver>` names for `pacman -Q`), recording the substitution. Knowledge-entry `recheck:` procedures prescribe no package-manager invocation; an older procedure is superseded by an appended steer-safe block. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-17.md` § Mistakes, R17-STEER-SCOPE; outside `round-16-verifier-spotcheck.md` § 5 item 3.
27. **Gate revisions name landed commits (R17-JJ-REVISION):** a gate `--revision` accepts only 7–64 hex resolved as `<hex>^{commit}` that is an ancestor of `refs/heads/<branch>` and, when present, of `refs/remotes/origin/<branch>`; `HEAD`, `@`, `@-` and ref names are rejected (`E-REVISION-INVALID`), unlanded commits `E-REVISION-NOT-LANDED`, unpushed `E-REVISION-NOT-PUSHED`, all exit 2; output carries `branch` and `branch_tip`. Under jj, git `HEAD` is the working-copy parent, not the branch. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-17.md` § Mistakes, R17-JJ-REVISION; outside `.ufo/waves/hyaku/jj-w1/reports/JjAuditTsukuroi.md` R1/R2 (fleet repo).
28. **One revision implementation, independent judge (R18-COPY-DIVERGENCE):** every lane gate imports `tools/_revision.py` for branch/revision checks; malformed branches precede revision errors, missing branches have their own id, merge-base errors are never non-ancestry, and first failure produces exactly one binding finding. Keep the measuring judge independent of that module and retain probes for all six round17 divergences; report original-nine continuity separately from newly discovered tools. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-18.md` § Mistakes; same evaluator rejects baseline842a531 (consistent1/9 despite bound9/9).
29. **Final evidence covers the final landing (R18-REPORT-TAIL):** a round's report names every late lane commit and reconciles delivered/dead seats from the final registry/report set, preserving interim outcomes as dated interim records. No remaining "running" label is silently carried as final completion. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-18.md` § Mistakes; outside round17 defects1/8.
30. **Whole-proposition capture locators (R18-BOOT-LOCATORS):** a plural image-equality statement cites each image's digest rows, and timestamps bind the actual image path rather than a same-hash history copy. Named count corrections include the exact counted argv or other finite input set. Old bytes remain with a named appended correction. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-18.md` § Mistakes; outside round17 defect3.

31. **Seat execution class is fixed at dispatch (R19-SEAT-DECLARE):** every dispatched item names its
execution class before delivery — `EXEC-AUTHORIZED` with the exact commands, or `NO-EXEC` with
read/grep/glob only and the literal trailer `Scope: executed=none`. A wave is green only when
`tools/check-seat-scope.py` at the landed revision reports zero `EXEC-UNAUTHORIZED`, `SCOPE-UNDECLARED`
and `TRAILER-MALFORMED` for build and falsify, and `REPORT-MISSING` only for seats recorded dead with
their provider outcome (QUALITY29). Authorization added after delivery,
reclassifying a disclosed command as read-only, or rewriting a trailer does not clear the seat; the
deviation stays recorded. Falsified by: dispatching any item with no class, or an `EXEC-AUTHORIZED`
item whose report lists an unlisted command, and the gate not exiting non-zero. Lesson:
`corpus/lessons/round-19.md` § Mistakes, R19-SEAT-DECLARE (scope); baseline red
`round-18-verifier-spotcheck.md:256-259` (build 37/1/4/2, falsify 9/0/0/2).
32. **Sibling ownership is cited by portable anchor, not line range (R19-ADJ-POINTERS):** each of the 10
outbound ownership pointers in `PROVINCE.md` (:33, :46-52) publishes the pinned sibling commit, the
exact heading, an exact ≤25-word quote and the sibling blob sha256; each inbound sibling pointer into
a lane file carries a found_line and a RESOLVES/DRIFTED/UNRESOLVABLE class. A bare `path:N-M` is not a
locator: it is red when the quote is absent from the pinned blob even if the range still holds text,
and a quote found at another line is DRIFTED and handed to the owner, never repaired in the sibling.
Falsified by: moving the quoted sentence one paragraph, or advancing the sibling commit — the anchor
must still resolve by quote plus blob while the line range alone misses it. Lesson:
`corpus/lessons/round-19.md` § Mistakes, R19-ADJ-POINTERS (reference-drift); live drift
`round-18-verifier-spotcheck.md:269-270` (mori `PROVINCE:66` → tsukuroi ADJ-04 now `PROVINCE:49`);
the drift warning already sits at `PROVINCE.md:70-73`.
33. **Known `/boot` writers are attributed before a write is a finding (R19-BOOT-ATTRIBUTION):** a boot-plane
audit reports a `/boot/limine.conf` write only with writer class, mechanism file:line, unit/hook
identity and event timestamp. Known scheduled writers are the `snapper-cleanup.service` drop-in
`ExecStopPost=limine-snapper-sync` (`host-capture-r19.txt:256-260`) under `snapper-cleanup.timer`
`OnUnitActiveSec=1h` (:221-231), the snapper plugin's transient `limine-snapper-add-*` `systemd-run`
units (`host-capture-r19b.txt:1093-1245`), and pacman's `90-mkinitcpio-install.hook` →
`limine-mkinitcpio-install` (`host-capture-r19.txt:346-409`; ALPM-SCRIPTLET writes at pacman.log 2021, 3015, 3047 = `host-capture-r19b.txt:1695,1700,1702`). A write matching a
known writer is expected automation, not evidence of interference; an unattributed unit, a failed
run, or a write without a matching timer or hook remains a finding. Falsified by: filing the hourly
10:07–17:07 `snapper-cleanup.service` writes (`host-capture-r19c.txt:13-20`) as unexplained, or the
audit still attributing those writes after the drop-in and timer are removed. Lesson:
`corpus/lessons/round-19.md` § Mistakes, R19-BOOT-ATTRIBUTION (attribution); round-18
recorded the rewrite as expected automation at `round-18-verifier-spotcheck.md:206-208,271-273`.

34. **Select and check the operative contract (R20-FALSE-OPERATIVE-MAP):** a repair assertion names the exact selected highest registry row, its entry mode and source return paths. Presence of digits0/2 is not absence of unsupported live1; live, selftest and usage codes are separate. A finite known-tool semantic check must reject the preserved prior row and historical-only repair before it counts. Disclose historical prefix exceptions from committed evidence; never call an in-place correction append-only. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-20.md` §Mistakes R20-FALSE-OPERATIVE-MAP/R20-SKILL-PREFIX-EXCEPTION; outside round19 §5 items2–3.
35. **Attribute declarations and localize supersession (R20-OUT009/R20-RULE4):** exact sibling package classes remain exact; a quoted “Owns” declaration is not an integrator ownership award or permission to write. Keep unresolved arbitration explicit. When old decision-rule wording overclaims an absence, append a named supersession identifying that rule item; no matching log event is not proof of no write or unchanged bytes. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-20.md` §Mistakes R20-OUT009/R20-RULE4; outside round19 §5 items4–5.
36. **Object identity before ref semantics; argv before binary labels (R20-HEX-REF/R20-SYSTEMCTL):** a hex selector must resolve actual object IDs before commit-ish peeling and ancestry checks; a hex-looking branch/tag name alone cannot bind. Preserve unique annotated-tag object peeling, uppercase and first-failure/origin controls. Exec-log systemctl classification must distinguish finite allowed read forms from mutation/unknown/effectful options without executing them; unsupported shell constructs remain explicit limitations. Independent expected outputs precede candidate outputs and must not import the binder under judgment. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-20.md` §Mistakes R20-HEX-REF/R20-SYSTEMCTL; outside round19 §5 items6–7.
37. **Follow BusyBox applet dispatch (R20-BUSYBOX-PARSER):** recurse into `-c` only for a known shell applet; an `echo -c` argument is inert, while `busybox sh -c` remains inspectable. Test both the inert control and mutation control, and classify a literal `busybox systemctl` through the finite systemctl policy. This is not shell equivalence: depth, aliases, expansion, redirection and dynamic sources remain unknown. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-20.md` §Mistakes R20-BUSYBOX-PARSER; falsifier `SysctlNestedShell.md`, finding 2.
38. **Bind reviews to candidate bytes (R20-CANDIDATE-SNAPSHOT):** each candidate read used for a verdict records the exact file SHA-256 and review predicate. Mutable worktree paths are not version identities; reports about different snapshots or predicates may coexist and cannot clear each other's findings. A final run binds only the integrated commit it actually measures. Lesson: `corpus/lessons/round-20.md` §Mistakes R20-CANDIDATE-SNAPSHOT; connector/falsify review disagreement in `ConnPlanner.md` §2 and `C1Prefix.md` §1.
